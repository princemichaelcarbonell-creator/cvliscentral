About CVLIS

Camp Vicente Lim Integrated School began in 1952 as a school for enlisted personnel at Camp Vicente Lim in Barangay Mayapa, Calamba, operating under the Armed Forces of the Philippines before it was turned over to the Department of Education and renamed Camp Vicente Lim National High School.

With the rollout of the K to 12 program, the school opened its Senior High School offering in 2016, and in 2017 it was renamed Camp Vicente Lim Integrated School to reflect its Junior and Senior High programs under one roof. Today it serves the Limian community as a public secondary school in Calamba City.

DepEd mandate

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education, in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.
